DRAPE MECHANISM (2026-07-18, completes the weight model): gowns/dresses failed the gate because fabric hanging between the feet/legs is >=7cm from ANY body surface, so K-NN has no mixture zone and assigns whole hems to one foot (70cm webs at scissor). Rule: loose cloth is suspended from the torso, not owned by the limb it floats near. Implementation in transfer_proxy_weights: drape factor = smoothstep of nearest-donor distance (_DRAPE_NEAR_M 0.04 -> _DRAPE_FAR_M 0.10) ramping the blended profile to Hips, then the factor is DIFFUSED 12 Jacobi iterations over the garment edge graph (vcount-aware polygons) so the ramp stays smooth where fabric flares faster than tessellation. A both-legs "bridge" rule was tried and reverted: measurably zero effect.

GATE FINAL FORM (validate.py, tessellation-fair): fail = fin OR web. fin = ratio>4 AND cur_len>0.20 AND bind_len<0.03 (fins are FINE geometry stretched LONG; coarse panels stretch broadly, not into spikes). web = ratio>10 AND cur_len>0.10 (every observed defect class >=11x and >=13.5cm; physical strain <=7x or <=9cm, e.g. jeans-crotch 13x on sub-mm edges is centimeter-scale). Calibrated on measured data, both rules are (ratio, absolute-size) pairs.
